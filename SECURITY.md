# Security

## What the mod touches

Brain Drain runs inside Claude Code on your own machine. The complete list of what it reads,
writes, removes and runs is in the README, under
[What it touches](README.md#what-it-touches).

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please report privately rather than in a public issue. Go to the
[Security tab of this repository](https://github.com/makingaipractical/brain-drain-for-claude-code/security)
and choose **Report a vulnerability**. That opens a private report that only the maintainer
can see. If you prefer email, send it to [info@makingaipractical.com](mailto:info@makingaipractical.com)
with "security" somewhere in the subject line.

Please include what the problem is, which version or commit, the steps to reproduce it, and
what an attacker could do with it. You do not need a proof-of-concept exploit; a clear
description is enough.

I aim to acknowledge a report within five working days; if you have heard nothing after that,
please send it again — it will have gone astray rather than been ignored. If the problem is
real, I will fix it as fast as I can, and the report stays private until the fix is out. I
cannot promise a fix by a particular date, and I do not pay for reports. I would rather say
that plainly than imply otherwise.

If you plan to write about the problem publicly, please give me time to fix it first. Ninety
days from your first report is the usual allowance; tell me your intended date and I will work
to it.
